# Typography

Two families, one scale, and a hard line between them.

- **Inter** carries everything you read — `--text-lg` and below.
- **Archivo** carries everything you scan — `--text-xl` and above. Archivo is a
  variable font, and Raqt uses it stretched — <code>font-stretch: {typography.display.stretch}</code>
  and <code>letter-spacing: {typography.display.tracking}</code>. That width is
  what makes a scoreline look like a scoreboard.

<Rule>
  **`font-display` is a utility, not a family.** It sets family, width and
  tracking together, so nothing can take Archivo without also taking the stretch.
  Applying `font-family: Archivo` by hand gives you Archivo at 100% width, which
  is a different typeface from the one Raqt uses.
</Rule>

## The two families side by side

The same line at `--text-2xl`, once in each family. The display face is wider and
tighter; that difference is the point of having two.

## What the fonts are

Both load from Google Fonts as variable fonts.

- `--font-sans` — <code>{typography.fonts.sans}</code>
- `--font-display` — <code>{typography.fonts.display}</code>

Weight is not a token: Inter is loaded across `400..700` and Archivo across
`400..800`, and components pick from that range with Tailwind's stock `font-*`
utilities. If a weight ever needs to *mean* something, it becomes a token first.
