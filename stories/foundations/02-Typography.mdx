# Typography

Two families, one scale, and a hard line between them. Shared across Player and
Referee — this is boxing, not brand.

- **Figtree** carries everything you read — `--text-lg` and below.
- **Space Grotesk** carries everything you scan — `--text-xl` and above, with
  <code>letter-spacing: {typography.display.tracking}</code>. That tracking is
  what the canvases use on the RAQT wordmark and scores.

<Rule>
  **`font-display` is a utility, not a family.** It sets family and tracking
  together, so nothing can take Space Grotesk without also taking the canvas
  tracking. Applying `font-family: Space Grotesk` by hand misses the tracking.
</Rule>

<Rule>
  **`2xs` and `3xs` are the caption floor, not a general small size.** They exist
  for mobile chrome the reading scale cannot carry: timeline stamps, seed
  numbers, avatar initials, bracket-stage labels. `3xs` is the floor and takes
  uppercase micro-labels only — never a sentence, never body copy. If a caption
  needs `3xs` to fit, the layout is usually the thing to change.
</Rule>

## The two families side by side

The same line at `--text-2xl`, once in each family. The display face is tighter;
that difference is the point of having two.

## What the fonts are

Both load from Google Fonts as variable fonts.

- `--font-sans` — <code>{typography.fonts.sans}</code>
- `--font-display` — <code>{typography.fonts.display}</code>

Weight is not a token: Figtree and Space Grotesk load across `400..700`, and
components pick from that range with Tailwind's stock `font-*` utilities. If a
weight ever needs to *mean* something, it becomes a token first.
