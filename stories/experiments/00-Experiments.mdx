# Experiments

Design explorations live here while their visual language and product behavior
are still being evaluated. They do not change the released RAQT theme or
component registry.

## Glow

The Glow exploration starts from the supplied mobile feed and extends it into
tournament discovery, next-match, scores, draw, messages, profile, achievements,
coaching, and live coverage. Use the screen selector above the phones to inspect
both themes together.

<Canvas of={GlowStories.CompareThemes} />

Open the stories under **Experiments → Glow** to view each screen individually,
change its theme, and interact with its controls.
